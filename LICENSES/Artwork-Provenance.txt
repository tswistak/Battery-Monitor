Battery Monitor logo and UI line-vector provenance

The Battery Monitor application logo is original artwork generated using
Codex. Its rounded battery shell, transparent opening and fixed partial
fill are used by the Android launcher, themed and notification icons.
The contained version in docs/assets/app-icon.svg is the source for
its documentation PNG and F-Droid metadata icon.

The logo is included with Battery Monitor's original artwork, copyright
2026 Tomasz Świstak, under the project's GPL-3.0-or-later terms, to the
extent of the project's rights.

The 45 ui_*.xml line-vector resources other than ui_fullscreen.xml were
generated for Battery Monitor using Codex.

These icons are included with Battery Monitor's original artwork under
the project's GPL-3.0-or-later terms, to the extent of the project's rights.
The complete GPLv3 text is packaged as GPL-3.0.txt.

The Material fullscreen glyph is covered separately by
Google-Material-Icons-Notices.txt and Apache-2.0.txt.
