Battery Monitor UI line-vector provenance

The 46 ui_*.xml line-vector resources other than ui_fullscreen.xml were
generated for Battery Monitor using Codex.

These icons are included with Battery Monitor's original artwork under
the project's GPL-3.0-or-later terms, to the extent of the project's rights.
The complete GPLv3 text is packaged as GPL-3.0.txt.

The Material fullscreen glyph and Material launcher battery are covered
separately by Google-Material-Icons-Notices.txt and Apache-2.0.txt.
