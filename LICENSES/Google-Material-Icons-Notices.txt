The battery symbol in ic_launcher_foreground.xml and
ic_launcher_monochrome.xml derives from the Google Material battery_full
symbol. Battery Monitor adapts its coordinates, scale, colors and shadow
for its application launcher icon.

ui_fullscreen.xml reproduces the Google Material fullscreen glyph in an
Android vector drawable. Its path commands have been reformatted without
changing the glyph geometry.

The complete Apache 2.0 license is packaged as Apache-2.0.txt.
