Battery Monitor is a simple, practical Android app for keeping an eye on your device’s battery.

See battery information at a glance, get useful alarms, and follow charging and discharging over time.

<b>⭐ Main features:</b>

- current battery readings and charging/discharging time estimates
- battery history with interactive charts
- configurable battery level, temperature and health alarms
- persistent notification and Live Updates on compatible Android 16+ devices
- configurable home screen widgets
- manual or automatic history export to CSV/JSON, and settings backup/restore
- optional advanced information through root or Shizuku
- local processing of battery and device information

<b>🔋 Current battery information</b>

See battery level, charging status, temperature, voltage, current and other readings available on your device. Check approximate charging and discharging times, including estimates to a battery level you choose.

<b>📲 Battery status notification</b>

Keep battery information visible in a persistent notification and choose which details it shows. On compatible devices running Android 16 or newer, you can also use Live Updates.

<b>🔔 Battery alarms</b>

Get notified when the battery becomes fully charged, rises above or drops below a level you choose, or reaches a high or low temperature. Alarms for health problems reported by Android are also available.

<b>📊 History and export</b>

Review charging and discharging over time with interactive charts and a filterable event history. Select a time range or compare two measurements on a fullscreen chart. Export history as CSV or JSON for further analysis, with automatic exports available too.

<b>🧩 Widgets</b>

Keep battery status, detailed readings or a recent trend on your home screen without opening the app. Choose the widget content and appearance that suit you.

<b>💾 Backup and restore</b>

Back up your settings, alarms and battery history, and restore them when needed.

<b>🎨 Appearance and compatibility</b>

Choose light, dark or true-black themes, with dynamic colors on supported devices. Battery Monitor requires Android 8.0 or newer. Root or Shizuku can provide additional readings on supported devices, but neither is required for everyday monitoring.

<b>🔒 Privacy</b>

Battery and device information is processed locally. Battery Monitor does not send monitoring data to external servers. Files you export or share may contain device information.

<b>📜 Project background</b>

Battery Monitor is a continued and rebranded version of Battery Indicator Pro / BatteryBot Pro, with ongoing development for modern Android versions.
