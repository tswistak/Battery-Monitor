Retained artwork includes stat_notify_alarm.png in the hdpi and xhdpi
resource directories and the inherited charging overlay used by the
charging*.png, charging_percentage*.png and charging_temp*.png status
icons. This fork regenerated the status-icon families with changed
typography and layout, and added percentage and temperature variants.
The charging overlay is extracted from the existing status icons by
tools/generate_numbers.rb and remains a modified derivative of the
upstream artwork. Modified derivatives retain CC BY-SA 3.0.

Modifications: Copyright © 2026 Tomasz Świstak.

Historical resources excluded from the current application

The original upstream LICENSE also identifies battery.png as based on the
Oxygen icon theme, under CC BY-SA 3.0. Those battery.png resources were
removed from this fork in commit 6df9b79cbf53af85ad68601b774b6e9b09035d34.

The upstream LICENSE identifies several Icons8 images under the historical
description "Free for open source". The identifiable clock, current,
health, temperature and voltage measurement images were removed in
commit 29d074fa9f26bca89089108044c0326158fd0bc5. No exact copies of the
original twelve Icons8 imports remain among current application PNGs.
This historical statement is not treated as a verified license grant for
any currently unidentified asset. Icons8 source: https://icons8.com/
